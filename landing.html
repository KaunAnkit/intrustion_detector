Core capabilities

Everything you need to secure a perimeter

Built on YOLO-based object detection and InsightFace embeddings, intrusion.ai delivers sub-second response times without cloud dependency.

Configurable Perimeter Line

Draw a custom boundary line directly on the camera feed. Define which side is the danger zone and let the system do the rest.

Real-time Intrusion Detection

Frames are analysed at 10Hz using YOLOv11. When a person crosses the boundary, an alert fires in under 100ms with a timestamped image capture.

Face Recognition

Register known faces once. intrusion.ai matches intruders against your database using cosine similarity on ArcFace embeddings.

Intrusion Log Dashboard

Search, filter, and review every recorded event. Each log entry includes the capture image, timestamp, and recognized identity.

Webhook Alarm Integration

Push intrusion status to any external alarm board or automation system via configurable webhook. Works with any HTTP endpoint.

Runs Fully On-Device

No cloud required. All inference happens locally, keeping your camera feed private and latency low regardless of network conditions.

How intrusion.ai works

From camera feed to identified intruder in four straightforward steps.

01

Connect your camera

Point intrusion.ai at any USB or IP camera. The live feed streams directly to the dashboard over a local network — nothing leaves your environment.

02

Draw your boundary

Use the interactive perimeter editor to place a line anywhere on the frame. Choose which side is restricted. The system immediately begins enforcing it.

03

Register known faces

Upload photos of authorized personnel. intrusion.ai generates face embeddings that persist across sessions and never require retraining.

04

Monitor and respond

Every boundary crossing is logged, photographed, and optionally forwarded to your alarm or notification system. Review, search, and export logs at any time.
